Privacy Policy — ShiftCore / MesaiKüpü

Last updated: 2026-10-21. Developer: Nexiva Studio. Contact: bunner48@gmail.com

What the app does with your data

  • Work records, wage settings, workplace location and shift settings are stored only on your device (local database). They are not uploaded to our servers. We do not run servers for this app and there is no account.
  • Location: if you set a workplace, the app uses your device location (including in the background if you allow "Allow all the time") only to detect when you arrive at or leave that workplace and to ask you to confirm a clock-in or clock-out. Location is processed on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Camera and motion/magnetic sensors: used on your device only by the measuring tools (spirit level, compass, camera measurement). Images and sensor values are not stored or sent.
  • Notifications: used to remind you and to ask for confirmations.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and usage data to show ads and measure performance, subject to your consent where required (for example in the EEA/UK) and Google's policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or disable your advertising ID in your device settings.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with Google (AdMob) as described above.

Your choices

You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. You can revoke location, camera and notification permissions at any time in system settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
